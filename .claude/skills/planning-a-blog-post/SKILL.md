# Planning a blog post

Plan with the maintainer, not for them. The post should have them in it, so ask before you outline.

## Step 1: ask (one message, three questions at most)

Pick from:
- Of everything on the list, what are you most excited to show?
- What was the most annoying or surprising part? (Good "behind the build" material.)
- Anything visual you already have, or a shot you'd love to see?
- Anything you want to tease, and how much can be said?

Skip a question if they've already answered it. Wait for the answers before step 2.

## Step 2: the plan, in this order

1. **Template and why** in one or two sentences, from the six in the `write-devlog` skill. A single small change is
   never its own post.
2. **Titles:** three to five, sentence case, a little markety, YouTube-ish but restrained: name the interesting
   thing, no fake urgency, no all caps, no clichés like "for real this time". Mark your pick.
3. **Sections** in order. For each: a sentence-case heading, the points it covers (from their list and answers), why
   a player would care, and the one visual it needs. Mark at most one section as the "behind the build" story.
4. **Room for you:** one or two places where their own take, story or joke would land best, phrased as a prompt to
   them ("your story about the half racks goes here"). Don't write jokes for them here.
5. **New visuals to make:** one per section, all fresh for this post (renders, GIFs, short clips, in-game captures),
   each described as a shot: subject, angle, what it should show. Existing media only as a deliberate callback. Offer
   to send the shot list to the mods session.

## Rules

- Only things from their list, their answers or the mod docs. Unreleased ideas stay vague.
- Hedge dates ("soon", "when it's ready"), never promise.
- Then offer the next step: they write and you edit (`editing-my-draft`), they send notes per section
  (`turning-notes-into-prose`), or you draft the whole post (`write-devlog`).
